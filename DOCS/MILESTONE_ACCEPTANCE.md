# Milestone acceptance criteria
Proposed numerical thresholds from audit p.12, not measured: reliable landmark mean error <=3% subject bounding-box diagonal; comparable visible silhouette IoU >=0.85; trusted contour error <=2% diagonal; topology preservation 100%; nonfinite vertices/introduced invalid faces 0; weight sum error <=0.0001; post-freeze MakeHuman accesses 0; target-switch body digest changes 0; manual JSON 0; unresolved garment intersections 0 within approved tolerance. Independent annotations, visible comparable regions only; report per-view and regional metrics, not clothed foreground as naked-body truth. Calibrate and approve before benchmark, never change thresholds after seeing results without a new baseline.
M1: R01 frontal authorized photo plus three-quarter and fragmented cases; automatic overlay, native fit demonstrably improves at least one supported anatomical metric over frozen bootstrap without degrading other approved tolerances; actual Blender import, editable mesh, armature, weights, UV, axes/units; no JSON; recovery. Generic template fails.
M2: same-person multi-view/different poses, held-out view not used in fit; compare same bootstrap and fixed evaluation protocol; improvement in held-out landmark/contour error or IoU without unacceptable regression; independent views, target switch digest invariant, revision recovery, Blender rig valid.
M3: supported poses including seated where approved, inspect joint deformation and contact, face where supported, UV/materials and portable textures in Blender, observed vs inferred coverage, neutral body preserved. No claim of identity likeness from silhouette alone.
M4: independent removable garments, supported fitting/deformation/layering, collision tolerance, portable complete USD, Blender edit/save/reopen/re-export and reimport checks; final privacy/license/security audit; UX scenarios UT01–12. Proposed UX targets from UX p.16: >=90% first-use completion and understanding inferred anatomy, >=85% recovery and error comprehension, <=5 core actions and one-click target, zero redundant confirmations/manual JSON/loss of work/unnecessary invalidation/misleading export. Recruit proposed 12 consenting participants across novice/photo-editing/3D experience; report counts and limitations. Test 1024x720 and 1440x900, keyboard and scaling. Record time to first alignment and fitted geometry separately; no SLA until measured.
Each gate requires executed full applicable tests, real-photo evidence and owner review. PARTIAL/FAIL/BLOCKED/NOT_RUN never PASS. Structural, photographic, anatomical plausibility and usability outcomes reported separately.

Hard invariants: zero stale-result promotion and lost valid revisions; topology preservation, no nonfinite/invalid geometry, zero post-freeze access, target-only digest change and mandatory JSON. Photographic thresholds remain provisional until independent calibration before evaluation; predeclare minimum meaningful held-out improvement, regional limits and supported visibility/pose envelope. Weight tolerance is numerical, not likeness. Garment tolerance and intentional contact defined before M4-001 completion. For 12 participants with one scored outcome each, 90% and 85% both require 11 successes; define denominators and scoring before testing. No scientific or population-wide claims.
