The global boundary cage uses tetrahedral piecewise-affine interpolation, with regularization on hull neighbors. It differs from the spline lattice in support, continuity across tetrahedra and control placement. Recovered displacement coefficients provide approximate deformation determinants in the fit logs. Regional cages, mean-value coordinates and refined boundary placement remain open.
