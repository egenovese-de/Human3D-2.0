Fixed reference planes cut the triangles into edge-connected loops. Arm, thigh and calf axes use reference joints on Family A and discard triangles outside a fixed same-side crop. Open or missing loops are unavailable. Chest loops are diagnostic because the arms can join the central torso loop. The machine-readable profile includes shape P95, width, depth and area. This remains a bounded cross-section study, with section orientation and repeatability still open.
