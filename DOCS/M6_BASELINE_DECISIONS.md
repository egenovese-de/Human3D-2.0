# M6 baseline decisions

## Evidence v2 and v1 migration

Use the existing photo-bound envelope, with v2 carrying typed observations alongside the legacy yaw and visibility fields. Old records, including records without an explicit version, remain readable as v1 and retain their original serialized form and dependency digests until replaced by an intentional observation revision. Provide an in-memory upgrade with an empty typed collection. Provider absence or rejection leaves detector evidence available. Bind the envelope and each field's provenance to the photo digest and candidate. Reject unsupported schema versions and stale attachments.

An observation identifies its region, measurement kind, versioned semantic definition, coordinate frame, units, value or closed interval, uncertainty, visibility and calibration reference when applicable. A semantic definition fixes endpoints, section planes, denominators and sign conventions. A missing value remains unknown. Region visibility is a fraction of the defined region in a specified view, whereas the provider's confidence is an uncalibrated score. Permit positive dimensionless proportions and circumference ratios where their denominator is defined. Require an independently established scale and uncertainty for metric observations. Preserve volume proxies as dimensionless descriptors and require valid closed surfaces for enclosed volume. Record signed asymmetry in anatomical left/right order.

Use an explicit allowlist of field definitions at each consumer. Validation and persistence alone grant no authority to affect fitting. Keep unsupported fields available for diagnostics. Add v2 consumers only with known-effect and conflict controls. Keep legacy yaw and Boolean visibility behavior until a specific replacement passes its gate. Correlated observations from the same photo/source family contribute as a group. Disagreement beyond the reported uncertainty or between incompatible calibrations keeps the conflicting evidence out of automatic fitting.

## Canonical landmarks and rig

Canonical space uses right-handed XYZ, with metric diagnostics normalized to metres, with Y up and positive Z forward. Map Blender XYZ into canonical axes with (X, Z, −Y), then apply explicit scale and ground translation to positions. Rotate external matrices by conjugating with the axis mapping. Store matrix rows in plain records and use column-vector algebra internally. Keep native lengths in decimetres and preserve USD export metadata (0.1 metres per stage unit). Convert matrices separately for USD conventions. In each rig, parent-relative rest and posed transforms are inverse(parent global) times child global. The rest-space local delta is inverse(rest local) times pose local. Skinning carries bind-space positions by posed global times inverse(bind global).

The release rebind derives joint centers from canonical vertex sets and estimates swing from projected segment directions. Treat surface landmarks and detector proxies as separate observations with their own semantic labels, visibility and uncertainties. Left/right refer to the subject. The source mapping in Q is qualified for the measured center/direction proxies. A cross-rig frame requires independently verified signed transverse axes in addition to head-tail direction before transferring axial twist, wrist/foot orientation or axial chains. Use positive right-hand angles and test both signs around each local axis, composed rotations, parent closure, geodesic local/global error, center distance in mm and foot contact. Where a frame is underdetermined, retain the existing pose path and mark the unavailable diagnostic.

## Benchmark protocol

Treat all published Q identities and variants as development or replay controls because their outcomes informed M6. Freeze new holdouts by underlying identity and generator/source family before candidate tuning, and keep all of an identity's poses, renders and photographs in the same partition. A family-independent gain requires a disjoint generation family. If none is qualified, report replay results and retain defaults whose promotion requires that holdout. Record input hashes, identity/family assignments and exclusions before scoring.

Use paired release/candidate observations with identical source bytes, evaluation cameras, alignment, sampling and resource budgets. Declare the candidate-specific minimum improvement and tolerance from development variability before unblinding the holdout. Keep safety and structural gates in ACCEPTANCE_M6.md mandatory. Aggregate paired effects by subject, include failures and denominators, and keep silhouette IoU, neutral symmetric surface mean/p95 in mm, angular geodesic error, and texture error/observed coverage separate. Classify touching independently from transverse or coplanar overlap. If sampling supports intervals, resample whole identities. Otherwise report individual paired effects and the observed range. Retain the release default when improvement or mandatory checks are unresolved.
