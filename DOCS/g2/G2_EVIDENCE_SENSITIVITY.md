G1 native controls were perturbed by ±0.04 at the available bounds. The matrix contains signed distances at baseline contour points, fixed-row projected profiles and a sampled interior metric-depth oracle. SVD and correlated columns are preserved. A lower relative rank after adding rows can result from an increase of the largest singular value or a different residual unit scale. It is not a loss of absolute information. Apparent height, overall transverse proportions, side depth and visible limb width generate useful signals. The latent age, weight/muscle, height/scale, chest/firmness, and posterior posture/contour confounds require priors, complementary data, or geometric reparameterization. Independent asymmetry and detailed face/hand recovery remain weakly qualified.
