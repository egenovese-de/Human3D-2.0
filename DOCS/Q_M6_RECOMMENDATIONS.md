# Q evidence-based M6 recommendations

| Priority | Component and measured limitation | Technical intervention | Complexity, dependencies and regression risk | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Limb pose. True-direction proxy substitution at fixed shape changed frontal overlap by +6.61 across 6 variants (range +4.37 to +8.71) percentage points. Relative direction estimation or mapping contributes to error under the pilot stance. | Add calibrated reference joint mapping and test pose confidence and limb depth jointly before algorithm changes. | Medium. Independent articulated benchmark and fixed 3D joints. Risk to wrists, relative depth and limbs behind the torso. | Paired neutral and articulated holdout with independent joint rotations and report angle, contact and silhouette separately. |
| P1 | Camera and mask coupling. Substitution effects in pp: mask -0.01 across 6 variants (range -0.21 to +0.22), camera -0.25 across 6 variants (range -3.44 to +3.24), interaction -0.01 across 6 variants (range -0.22 to +0.21). Camera error and compensating pose/shape are suspected controls. | Validate view calibration and separate segmentation confidence from camera quality before modifying the solver. | Medium. Depends on repeatable camera models and detector-bound evidence. Risk of worse heavily cropped cases. | Repeat the four-way factorial with perspective, view-angle coverage and held-out validation. |
| P1 | Geometry capacity. Direct-fit and calibrated surface errors are measured in the geometry table. All 12 runs used one frozen base. The male comparison exposes a chest profile discrepancy and the automatic search lacks a base-shape selector. Stance alignment remains a confound for the aggregate error. Population improvement cannot be estimated from two topology families. | Establish an articulated registration oracle and expand independent rest-shape coverage before adding bounded regional controls. | High. Correspondence, rig rebinding, UV and garment deformation are dependencies. Existing proportions and support-plane behavior can regress. | Same alignment protocol and held-out subjects with registered joints plus calibration. Measure the change at fixed objective and fixed bounds. |
| P2 | View utility. Front/profile and front/rear held-out deltas +0.53 across 6 variants (range +0.22 to +0.83) and +0.00 across 6 variants (range +0.00 to +0.00) pp. Appearance coverage benefits from extra views. | Evaluate view quality weighting and redundant-view rejection before making more photographs the default. | Medium. Requires matched exposure, camera metadata and contradictory/duplicate fixture families. Risk of dropping genuinely complementary evidence. | Paired held-out usage and UV coverage, with marginal latency and failure-rate reporting. |

The ranges in this table are observed pilot variation. No P0 classification is supported by the completed controlled comparisons. No M6 work is implemented.
