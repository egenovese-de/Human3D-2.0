# Fixture and evaluation data policy

Public CI admits only original procedural fixtures with generator revision, deterministic seed where applicable, SHA-256, license and reviewer disposition recorded before admission. No legacy photographs, personal likenesses, derived private meshes, EXIF, credentials or private paths. Third-party fixtures require separate explicit redistribution clearance. Default deny.

Private empirical inputs remain outside the repository and CI, on authorized local storage. Record consent or other applicable authorization, scope, retention and deletion rules locally; never publish subject identifiers, photographs or derived assets. Public reports contain only nonidentifying aggregate metrics and test status. Do not treat synthetic results as empirical evidence.

## Admission register
No public test fixtures currently admitted. Each future entry must identify fixture ID, generator/source, revision, digest, license, privacy review and disposition. Reject missing evidence. No private data in CI logs, caches or artifacts.
