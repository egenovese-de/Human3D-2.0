# Q final blocker disposition

All nine tasks retain **BLOCKED** in the backlog. A research disposition documents the strongest supported conclusion and can coexist with that status. The source baseline is v0.2.0, commit `480195f05fb2d1ed6c71dceabf4ba54b8bed69db`.

| Task and disposition | Original requirement | Work and measured results | Remaining dependency, alternatives and readiness impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q2-004 · INSUFFICIENT_VALIDATED_EVIDENCE | Calibrated detector-equivalent truth for 33 landmarks, with visibility, uncertainty and oracle ablation. | Independently defined 12 joint-center proxies across 48 views. Tested 576 projections and matched native/proxy alignment. Silhouette IoU averaged 55.20% native and 58.84% with observed XY proxies. Added 26 facial picks across two identities and two views. | A separate reviewer, surface/joint convention validation, the remaining eye, ear, digit, heel and toe points, synthetic occlusion truth and release world-coordinate calibration are still needed. Reusing native coordinates or treating skin-ray occlusion as subsurface-joint occlusion would bias validation. Planning can establish the measurement contract. Full landmark accuracy and pose replacement remain gated. |
| Q2-005 · VERIFIED_ARCHITECTURAL_LIMITATION | Complete anatomy oracle through the released provider plus supported field and consumer validation. | Replayed 16 view cases. Executed 27 binding/yaw/visibility boundary checks and eight fixed-input appearance bakes. Hidden-arm coverage dropped about 2.20 pp at the confidence threshold. The cross-consumer mapping is in the finalization report. | The typed schema exposes only yaw and Boolean regional visibility, with confidence for those signals. No independent measurement fields or downstream hooks exist for the requested quantitative anatomy. Range/threshold and consumer probes are complete. Supporting more evidence requires an M6 contract decision. |
| Q2-007 · INSUFFICIENT_VALIDATED_EVIDENCE | Joint oracle spanning segmentation, camera, validated landmarks, full pose and anatomical evidence. | Replayed 16 calibrated-camera and raster-mask cells with native pose or independent direction proxies. At fitted shape, direction proxy improved held-out IoU 60.24% → 66.16% and posed error 34.24 → 26.11 mm. Separate XY joint and provider interventions were validated within their stated limits. | A calibrated full 3D detector-equivalent landmark set, a full cross-rig rotation mechanism and quantitative provider anatomy remain unavailable. Direction and separate provider probes quantify partial effects. Combining unverified fields would leave the same causal ambiguity. Plan full oracle support alongside the pose and evidence contract. |
| Q2-008 · VERIFIED_REPRESENTATION_LIMITATION | Independently registered geometry through fixed pose, texture and garment contracts. | Four subjects, five transfer conditions each. Exact canonical controls passed. All eight nominal 12/24 mm transfers failed native face-area validation. Both damped levels passed on all four subjects with canonical topology, UV and skin preserved. The earlier repaired independent layered import remains an editability control. | Anatomically verified finger and face correspondence, common UV/material mapping, bind/weight correspondence and a shape-preserving registered transfer are needed. Further damping only tests compatibility at weaker transfer magnitudes. The failure is specific to the tested transfers. Decide canonical representation and registration before interpreting downstream oracle gaps. |
| Q5-001 · INSUFFICIENT_VALIDATED_EVIDENCE | Face landmark, profile and regional likeness errors against independently registered anatomy. | All 26 single-review surface picks hit the mesh. Fourteen comparable detector observations gave exploratory 0.27–6.80 px differences. One-pixel ray sensitivity reached 20.18 mm at surface transitions. Prior width grid measured 10.98 → 10.32 mm mean cranial error. | Independent review and matched native/reference face anchors are still needed. Texture appearance and width-envelope scores cannot establish 3D identity likeness. Nose, cheek and expression axes are absent from the release. Decide needed controls and attach blinded feature benchmarks. |
| Q5-007 · VERIFIED_REPRESENTATION_LIMITATION | Cloth fit, boundaries, posed intersections and defensible volume including open-mesh caps. | Replayed 18 neutral comparisons. Added 12 native posed conditions and 24 cap screens. Eight posed shell cases had zero sampled violations and 34 triangle pairs each. Both centroid fans and polygon tessellation failed all native cap screens. | There is no valid closed shell for these candidate constructions and no thickness/material-domain annotation. Report native volume as unavailable. A later envelope proxy would need validated orientation, closed connected components and intersection-free caps; material volume would require thickness. P1 collision validation can proceed independently. |
| Q7-002 · INSUFFICIENT_VALIDATED_EVIDENCE | Freeze acceptance rule and evaluate beneficial, harmful and negligible native fits on distinct identities. | Replayed the 12 development and eight original evaluation configurations and added articulated native input screening under the frozen rule. Of eight articulated cases, two were beneficial, two harmful and four negligible by the frozen held-out IoU label. The rule rejected all eight. The two IoU-beneficial changes increased neutral surface error by 0.58 and 0.05 mm. A 0.18 mm decrease in neutral error coincided with harmful IoU. | The original rule optimizes silhouette utility. Pose-dependent IoU gains can coincide with worse neutral surface. An independent source-family evaluation and a frozen geometry-focused rule with regional/LOVO features are still needed. The final benchmark gives the full screening denominators. |
| Q7-003 · INSUFFICIENT_VALIDATED_EVIDENCE | Full rotation mapping and comparison of camera/pose orders, alternating refinement, and subsequent fit. | Replayed 32 fixed-evaluation cells. Source chain closure on 12 fixtures and 18 named bones per fixture had maximum matrix-entry residual 1.19e−7. Earlier work measured 27.82° mean rotation-change discrepancy and tested a second camera/shape pass with native pose. | Rest-frame offsets, axial limb twist, wrist/foot axes and spine/pelvis cross-rig mapping remain unverified. Pose-first and full alternating comparisons depend on those conventions. Direction replacement establishes sensitivity but does not identify the full residual. Plan rig mapping before algorithm ordering. |
| Q7-004 · INSUFFICIENT_VALIDATED_EVIDENCE | Identity-aware selection plus matched differentiated geometry and appearance evaluation. | Replayed 14 reference treatments with four incompatible inputs accepted by both heuristic policies. Replayed matched and mixed geometry outputs, refit geometry with only the suspect profile omitted, and crossed both geometries with both appearance sets. Omitting the profile from appearance bakes cost 5.10 pp mean UV coverage. Omitting it from geometry changed held-out IoU by 0 to +0.46 pp and neutral surface error by 0 to +0.73 mm. | Registered matched-color truth and a validated identity criterion are still required. Yaw/visibility tests and differentiated rebakes bound the existing heuristics. Plan identity evaluation before production reference filtering. |
